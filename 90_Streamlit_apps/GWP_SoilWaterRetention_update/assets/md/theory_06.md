Molecules at the interface form a thin, *membrane-like* layer (**Figure 4**) that adjust their geometry to minimize the occupied surface area. Interfacial tension is defined as force per unit length _N_/_m_, and can be expressed as interfacial energy per unit area _N_/_m²_ $N$/$m^{2}$, using the same numerical value. An expansion of the interfacial surface creates more surface area, requiring a proportional amount of work.
