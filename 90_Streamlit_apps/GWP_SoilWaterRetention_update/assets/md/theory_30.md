- **$K_s$** (*Saturated hydraulic conductivity*): The rate at which water moves through fully saturated soil under a unit gradient, expressed in cm/day or mm/h.
- **$K_r$** (*Relative hydraulic conductivity*): The fraction of $K_s$ that prevails at a given moisture content. Two equivalent expressions exist: in terms of $\Theta$ and in terms of $h$.
