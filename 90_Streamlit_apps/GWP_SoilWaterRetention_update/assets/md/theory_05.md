**Interfacial tension** $\sigma_{ik}$ is a constant for a given pair of substances $i$ and $k$. It can be be understood as the *force* required to prevent mixing of the fluids by overcoming the **cohesive forces** between the molecules of each substance. In other words, it is equivalent to the energy needed to **separate a unit area** of substance $i$ from substance $k$, effectively increasing the surface area of their interface. This force resists mixing of the fluids resulting in a surface between the fluids—and that resistance is what we call **surface tension** (**Figure 3**).
