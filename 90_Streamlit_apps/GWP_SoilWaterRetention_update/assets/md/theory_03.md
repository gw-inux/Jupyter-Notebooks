Why can water rise in fine soil pores, defying gravity? What determines whether water coats soil grains or forms droplets?
    
At the pore scale, curved interfaces form between the wetting and non-wetting fluids due to cohesive and adhesive forces, creating menisci that generate capillary pressure. Wettability — governed by surface tension and contact angle — controls how water enters, moves through, and is retained in the unsaturated soil.
