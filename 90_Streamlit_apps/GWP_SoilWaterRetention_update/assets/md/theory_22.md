In the context of unsaturated soils, $\theta$ ranges between the residual water content ($\theta_r$), below which water is no longer mobile or plant-accessible, and the saturated water content ($\theta_s$), where all pores are filled with water.
 
Understanding how $\theta$ varies with pressure head (i.e., suction) is key to modeling soil-water behavior, especially for predicting plant-available water and infiltration dynamics. The van Genuchten model represents this relationship through a nonlinear function that describes the soil-water-retention curve.
