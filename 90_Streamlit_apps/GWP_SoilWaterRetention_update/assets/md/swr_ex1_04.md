In this exercise, you explored how to fit van Genuchten model parameters to measured soil retention data for two contrasting soils. By adjusting the parameters $\theta_r$, $\theta_s$, $\alpha$, and $n$, you gained insight into how each one affects the shape and steepness of the soil water retention curve.

You also developed hands-on calibration skills and visually assessed the model fit—a fundamental task in soil physics and unsaturated zone hydrology.

In practice, fitting van Genuchten parameters to field data typically involves optimization techniques. This visual fitting exercise builds the essential intuition needed to understand how retention behavior varies across soil types.
