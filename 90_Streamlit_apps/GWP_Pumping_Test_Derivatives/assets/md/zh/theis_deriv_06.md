#### Variation of Transmissivity

In this investigation, storativity remains constant while transmissivity is varied. Explore how drawdown and derivative responses change and identify which characteristics of the pumping-test response are controlled by transmissivity.

**Questions to consider**
- How does transmissivity affect drawdown?
- How does transmissivity affect the derivative plateau?
- Which parts of the response are most sensitive to changes in transmissivity?
- Why does the derivative plateau provide information about transmissivity?

The goal of this exercise is to develop an intuitive understanding of how transmissivity influences drawdown and derivative behavior.
