#### A First Look at Derivatives

This example illustrates the relationship between drawdown and its derivative for ideal Theis conditions. Use the sliders to modify transmissivity and storativity and observe how both curves respond.

**Questions to consider**
- How does transmissivity affect drawdown and the derivative plateau?
- How does storativity influence the timing of the response?
- Why does the derivative approach a constant value at late time?

The goal of this exercise is to develop an intuitive understanding of derivative behavior before working with pumping-test data.
