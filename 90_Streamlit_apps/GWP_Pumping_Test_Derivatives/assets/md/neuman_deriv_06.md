#### Single Neuman Curve

In this investigation, all Neuman parameters can be adjusted interactively. Explore the drawdown and derivative response of an unconfined aquifer and identify the characteristic features associated with delayed water-table drainage.

**Questions to consider**
- Which parts of the response resemble the behavior of a confined aquifer?
- When does delayed water-table drainage become visible?
- How does the transition between early-time and late-time behavior appear in the derivative?
- Why can the derivative reveal flow-regime changes more clearly than the drawdown curve?

The goal of this exercise is to develop an intuitive understanding of the characteristic drawdown and derivative response of pumping tests in unconfined aquifers.
