With the following interactive investigations, we explore drawdown and drawdown derivatives for pumping tests in confined aquifers. All examples are based on the Theis solution and illustrate the influence of transmissivity and storativity on aquifer response.

The derivative provides an additional diagnostic view of the pumping test. Under ideal Theis conditions, it approaches a constant plateau that represents infinite-acting radial flow.
