This section focuses on pumping tests in semi-confined aquifers and the interpretation of drawdown derivatives using the Hantush solution. The Hantush solution extends the Theis solution by accounting for leakage through a semi-confining layer into the pumped aquifer.

#### 💡 Motivation - Why study the Hantush solution and its derivative?

Many aquifers are not perfectly confined. Leakage through overlying or underlying layers can significantly influence pumping-test responses and lead to deviations from ideal Theis behavior. Understanding how leakage affects drawdown and derivative responses is essential for identifying semi-confined conditions and estimating leakage characteristics.

#### 🎯 Learning objectives

After completing this section, you should be able to:

- Explain how leakage influences drawdown and derivative responses.
- Describe the physical meaning of the leakage factor and the parameter r/B.
- Recognize characteristic deviations from the Theis derivative plateau caused by leakage.
- Distinguish between confined and semi-confined aquifer responses.
- Estimate aquifer and leakage parameters from pumping-test data.

The section combines analytical solutions and synthetic pumping-test data.

1. Explore drawdown and derivative responses predicted by the Hantush solution.
