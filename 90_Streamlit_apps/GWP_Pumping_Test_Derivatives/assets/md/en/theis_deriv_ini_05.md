With the following interactive plots, we explore drawdown and drawdown derivatives for a pumping well. The figure illustrates an ideal pumping test in a confined aquifer. Water is pumped from a well at a constant rate of 1000 m³/d, and drawdown is observed at a monitoring well located 100 m from the pumping well. The pumping well is situated within an idealized groundwater catchment receiving recharge and connected to a lake. These features may influence the pumping response and can produce characteristic signatures in drawdown-derivative plots.

The derivative provides an additional diagnostic view of the aquifer response. Under ideal Theis conditions, it approaches a constant plateau that represents infinite radial flow.
