#### Effect of Beta

In this investigation, transmissivity, specific storage, and specific yield remain constant while the Neuman beta parameter is varied. Explore how beta influences the transition between early-time and late-time behavior.

**Questions to consider**
- How does beta influence the timing of the transition zone?
- How does beta affect the shape of the derivative curve?
- Which parts of the response are most sensitive to changes in beta?
- Why can beta provide insight into vertical flow processes within the aquifer?

The goal of this exercise is to develop an intuitive understanding of how the beta parameter influences drawdown and derivative behavior in unconfined aquifers.
