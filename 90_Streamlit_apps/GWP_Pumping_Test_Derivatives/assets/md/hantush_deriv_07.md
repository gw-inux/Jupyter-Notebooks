#### Variation of Storativity

In this investigation, transmissivity remains constant while storativity is varied. Explore how drawdown and derivative responses change and identify which characteristics of the pumping-test response are controlled by storativity.

**Questions to consider**
- How does storativity affect drawdown?
- How does storativity influence the timing of the response?
- Does storativity affect the derivative plateau?
- Which parameter appears to control the horizontal position of the curves?

The goal of this exercise is to develop an intuitive understanding of how storativity influences the timing of pumping-test responses.
