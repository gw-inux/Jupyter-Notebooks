#### Effect of Specific Yield

In this investigation, transmissivity, specific storage, and the beta parameter remain constant while specific yield is varied. Explore how delayed water-table drainage influences drawdown and derivative responses.

**Questions to consider**
- Which parts of the response are most affected by specific yield?
- How does specific yield influence late-time drawdown?
- How does specific yield influence the derivative transition zone?
- Why is specific yield often estimated from late-time pumping-test behavior?

The goal of this exercise is to develop an intuitive understanding of how specific yield controls delayed drainage and late-time aquifer response.
