#### Effect of Transmissivity

In this investigation, specific storage, specific yield, and the beta parameter remain constant while transmissivity is varied. Explore how drawdown and derivative responses change and identify which characteristics of the pumping-test response are controlled by transmissivity.

**Questions to consider**
- How does transmissivity affect drawdown?
- How does transmissivity affect the derivative level?
- Which parts of the response are most sensitive to changes in transmissivity?
- Why does the derivative provide valuable information for estimating transmissivity?

The goal of this exercise is to develop an intuitive understanding of how transmissivity influences drawdown and derivative behavior in unconfined aquifers.
