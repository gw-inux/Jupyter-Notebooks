With the following interactive investigations, we explore drawdown and drawdown derivatives for pumping tests in unconfined aquifers. All examples are based on the Neuman solution and illustrate the influence of transmissivity, specific yield, and the beta parameter on aquifer response.

The derivative provides an additional diagnostic view of the pumping test. In unconfined aquifers, the derivative often reveals the transition from elastic aquifer storage to delayed water-table drainage more clearly than the drawdown curve alone.
