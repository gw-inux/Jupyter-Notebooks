**Dr.-Ing. Thomas Reimann** is a researcher and lecturer at the **Institute of Groundwater Management, TU Dresden, Germany**. His work connects groundwater engineering, numerical modeling and digital education.

He earned his engineering diploma in 2003 and doctorate in 2012, studying karst groundwater systems, and contributes to the development of MODFLOW's Conduit Flow Process. He has taught at TU Dresden since 2003 and develops interactive materials including the soil-water retention and [boundary-condition modules](https://gwp-boundary-conditions-intro.streamlit.app/).
