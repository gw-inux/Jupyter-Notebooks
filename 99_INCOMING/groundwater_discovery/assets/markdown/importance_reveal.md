**About 99%** is groundwater in the rounded global estimate used by The Groundwater Project. This is a statement about **stored liquid freshwater**, not annual supply or water ready to drink. Ice contains most of Earth's total freshwater.

Why does this hidden store matter? It supplies wells and can sustain rivers and wetlands between rainfall events. Its large volume does **not** mean unlimited usable water: some is inaccessible, poorly replenished or contaminated. Protecting groundwater also protects connected surface waters. [S1, S3]
