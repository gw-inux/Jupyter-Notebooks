Freshwater is less dense than saltwater. In a suitable coastal aquifer it can form a **freshwater body above saline groundwater**. This illustration shows an ideal island lens, including the land surface and seabed.

Compare the four water levels. **If the freshwater level falls, what happens to the interface?** Pumping can lower that level and draw saltwater toward wells. This comparison explains the tendency; it does not predict a pumping rate or response time. [S6, S8, S12]
