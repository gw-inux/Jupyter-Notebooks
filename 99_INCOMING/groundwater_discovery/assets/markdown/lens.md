Rain falling on an island can infiltrate and recharge its aquifer. Because freshwater is less dense than saltwater, it can form a **freshwater lens above saline groundwater**. Continued recharge feeds the lens while groundwater flows toward the sea. [S12, S15, S16]

Imagine a circular island with an area of **1 km²**. Its freshwater level depends on recharge, the size of the island and how readily water moves through its materials. Here the material and area stay fixed: a higher natural level represents greater recharge. Compare levels from **0.1 to 1.0 m above sea level**, then add a central pumping well.
