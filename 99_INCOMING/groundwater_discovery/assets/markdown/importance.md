Oceans make Earth look rich in water, but most of that water is salty. Much of its freshwater is frozen in ice. To understand groundwater's role, we will compare the remaining **liquid freshwater stores**, rather than all the water on Earth. A large store does not automatically mean water is easily accessible or safe to drink. [S1, S2]

**Think of 100 equal units of liquid freshwater on Earth.** How many are underground? Make a prediction before revealing the estimate.
