**Wet sand is not the same as the water table.** Capillary forces can keep sand wet above it. Waves, changing sea levels, recharge and local layers can alter the pattern; the sea is not actually constant. Beach pore water may be fresh, brackish or salty. Its level alone does not identify its salinity or safety to drink.

The connection works both ways: groundwater can feed a river or the sea; surface water can also enter the ground. For connected freshwater systems, flow is driven from higher to lower hydraulic head. Coastal flow additionally depends on water density. [S5, S10, S12]
