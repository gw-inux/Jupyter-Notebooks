1. Fill a transparent measuring cup to a marked level with clean, dry glass beads or smooth non-absorbent gravel. Record the **bulk volume**, including the gaps.
2. Predict how much water will fit. Add a measured volume slowly until water just reaches the top of the grains; gently tap to release air. Do not add a water layer above them.
3. For clean, non-absorbent grains without internal pores, estimate **solid volume = bulk volume − water added**. Enter bulk and solid volumes in the calculator above. Explain where the water went.

Peas or lentils can illustrate the gaps, but they absorb water and swell: use them only for a quick qualitative demonstration. Even with beads, trapped air, spills and packing changes affect the estimate. This measures accessible pore space approximately, not drainable storage. Do not infer that all stored water can be pumped out. [S4]
