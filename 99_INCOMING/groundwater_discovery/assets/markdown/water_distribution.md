Read the two columns from left to right. **Freshwater is about 2.5% of all water on Earth.** Of that freshwater, about 68.7% is in glaciers and ice caps, 30.1% is groundwater and 1.2% is surface or other freshwater. The figures are rounded. [S18]

**The total being compared matters.** Here, 30.1% refers to *all freshwater, including ice*. The earlier ≈99% estimate refers to *liquid freshwater*. These are different totals; neither percentage tells us how much water is readily available or safe to drink. [S1]
