The content draws on [Groundwater Project books](https://gw-project.org/books/), available to read and download free of charge. Numbered references accompany the learning sections, and external illustrations are credited separately.

The design follows the related soil-water retention and [boundary-condition modules](https://gwp-boundary-conditions-intro.streamlit.app/): brief explanations, interactive figures, reflection and multilingual learning. Diagrams simplify natural systems for education; they are not site-specific predictions.
