1. Most water is salty, and much freshwater is frozen. Of Earth's **liquid freshwater**, about 99% is groundwater. This is a storage estimate, not the share we can safely extract or drink: depth, quality, ecosystems and replenishment also matter. [S1]
2. Infiltrating water enters pores. Some remains above the water table or returns to the atmosphere; some reaches the saturated zone as recharge. **Porosity** is pore volume divided by total material volume. It describes space available for water, not how much water is always present or can drain out. [S6, S7]
3. At a connected beach, groundwater and seawater levels meet near the shore. Inland levels may be slightly higher. A gaining river receives groundwater from higher levels; a losing river supplies groundwater at lower levels. [S10, S13]
