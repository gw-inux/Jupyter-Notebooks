[The Groundwater Project](https://gw-project.org/) makes groundwater understandable through free **[books](https://gw-project.org/books/), [videos](https://gw-project.org/videos/) and [interactive educational materials](https://gw-project.org/interactive-education/)**. Its resources serve children, the general public, students and specialists. The examples below offer different starting points; each book page includes available translations.
