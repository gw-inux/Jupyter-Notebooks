This simpler water-cycle sketch follows one route from a cloud, through the ground and back to surface water. Look first at the arrows above the land, then at those below it. Water entering the soil is **infiltration**; the part that reaches the water table becomes **groundwater recharge**. Some infiltrated water returns to the air through evaporation or plants instead. [S9]

The sun supplies energy for **evaporation**. Water vapour cools and condenses into cloud droplets; precipitation returns water to the surface. Some water soaks down far enough to **recharge groundwater**. Groundwater moves through connected pores and may return to a river or the sea. Here, surface water receives the flow. [S10, S9]
