Tell a partner, in your own words:

1. Earth has plenty of water. Why is only a small part readily available as freshwater for people? Where is most of Earth's **liquid freshwater** stored?
2. Where does water that infiltrates underground go? Which characteristic of a porous material describes the fraction of its volume that can hold water?
3. Why might a beach hole contain water near sea level? How could groundwater levels differ beside a gaining or losing river?

**Questions for further thinking:** Where does your drinking water come from, and which river, wetland or coastal area might be connected to it? Investigate using a reliable local source. There is no universal answer.
