**Quick demonstration (5–7 min):** ask for a freshwater estimate; fill one cup; reveal the beach water table; finish with one takeaway and a Groundwater Project link.

**Classroom (20–30 min including discussion):** 3 min predict and discuss water stores; 7 min cup experiment; 7 min beach investigation; 3–5 min explain and check. Add time for a real outdoor activity.

Use **predict → observe → explain**. Invite reasons before revealing answers. Let partners disagree, test and revise. Keep equations and the freshwater lens optional. Read prompts aloud when useful; all graphics have accompanying text. Quizzes are formative, with retries and no ranking. Correct-answer counts are session-only practice indicators, not evidence of certified competence.
