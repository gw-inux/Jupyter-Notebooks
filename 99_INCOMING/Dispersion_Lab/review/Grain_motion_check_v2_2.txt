DISPERSION LABORATORY 2.2 — CHECK OF SLOW AND UPWARD MOTION
30 October 2026

Question
Can the reported slow or locally upward particle motion arise from the existing
pressure-driven grain-network model, or from incorrect trajectory interpolation?

Configuration
Config(mode='grains', rows=20 or 40, particles=10010), with the other defaults:
a=1 mm, grain-size factor 0.75, grain variation 0.25, imposed pressure difference
0.5 Pa, grain seed 7, particle seed 42, instantaneous point injection.
There are 10 highlighted + 10,000 independently sampled background particles.
All markers have mass 1/10010. Display stretching and screen duration do not
enter any calculation below.

The companion grain_motion_v2_2.json retains the unrounded computed values.

Checks and interpretation
- Every sampled transition decreased calculated node pressure strictly and had
  positive finite travel time. No particle revisited a node; every path ended at
  an outlet. Waiting + active + collected particle counts conserve total mass.
- Routing follows outgoing water flow Q_ij = g_ij (P_i - P_j), not the sign of
  the plotted vertical increment. Heterogeneous conductances permit a neighbor
  with lower pressure to lie locally above the current node. Strictly decreasing
  pressure rules out a cycle in this steady network. Local upward motion is
  therefore retained, with a visible explanation in the app.
- Connection time is pi*r^2*L/abs(Q). Small nonzero flow yields long finite travel
  times. With many markers, a rare slow path can set the animation's final time;
  most particles then arrive early on that same linear display clock.
- Regression checks evaluate positions at 25% and 75% of an actual upward leg
  and of the slowest sampled leg. Both match linear interpolation of the computed
  endpoints and show a nonzero displacement. The slow motion is not a numerical
  interpolation freeze in these cases.
- The grain display now uses the same vertical scale factor of two as the
  pinboard. This stretches drawn grains; coordinates, hydraulic radii, flow,
  routing probabilities, and all model times are unchanged.
- The pathway table exposes node pressure and the time to the next node, so the
  learner can inspect both effects with Pause and Previous/Next step.

Reproduction
python -m pytest -q tests/test_experiment.py -k grain_upward_and_slow
The tests exercise both complete cohorts and the above physical invariants.
The existing numerical model source is byte-for-byte identical to v2.1 after
normalizing only the exported version string.

Scientific scope
The formulation is an idealized steady resistor/throat network. Plot z is a
flowwise distance coordinate, not a separately included elevation-head term.
No pore-body storage, trapping, sorption, molecular diffusion, or collision model
has been introduced. Conservation and interpolation checks verify this model's
implementation; they do not validate it for a measured porous medium.
Primary formulation reference: OpenPNM, Simulating transport
https://openpnm.org/examples/tutorials/08_simulating_transport.html
The app has its own solver; it does not depend on OpenPNM.
