1. **Principles and guided experiment:** follow one particle through a fixed board; compare its outlet and arrival time with a few more particles.
2. **Pinboard experiment:** increase the sample with blue background markers. Change rows, spacing, and fixed or variable travel times one at a time.
3. **Grain network:** explore how idealized pore openings and heterogeneity affect water flow and solute transport.
4. **References and model notes:** examine equations, assumptions, and limits when you need more detail.

The models are teaching analogies. Neither is calibrated to a particular soil, and neither resolves molecular diffusion.
