Independent hydraulic and travel-time audit
Command: python scripts/audit_travel_times.py OUTPUT_JSON
Result: exit code 0. Data: review/travel_time_audit_v2_4.json.
Nine hydraulic configurations span 2–40 rows, grain factors 0.5–0.9, variability
0–0.4, pressures 0.05–2 Pa and two grain seeds. An independent dense solve was
compared to the implemented sparse solve; equivalent L/velocity transit times,
branch flow fractions, total probability and flowing-volume/inlet-flow mean
transit were checked. Worst relative discrepancies:
- Pressure / inlet pressure: 3.00e-15.
- Flow / inlet flow: 5.08e-15.
- Equivalent connection-time formulas: 6.66e-16.
- Internal nodal mass residual: 5.47e-15.
- Inlet/outlet imbalance: 1.52e-14.
Three complete cohorts of 10,010 particles each had positive finite connection
times, strictly falling pressure, no revisited nodes, valid outlet termination
and conserved mass. These are representative/boundary checks, not an exhaustive
parameter sweep or field validation.

In the default 20-row sample (grain seed 7, particle seed 42), median arrival
was 423.18 s, the 95th percentile 771.87 s and latest arrival 5,897.22 s. The
latest path had no upward steps: one downward, very-low-pressure-drop connection
accounted for 90.23% of its transit time. Slow flow, rather than repeated cycling,
explains this late arrival. The 60-second clock displays its 95th percentile at
about 7.85 s. Exact values and other samples are in Travel_Time_Review_v2_4.md.

Environment and limits
Python 3.12; Streamlit 1.50.0. Playwright 1.51.0 / Chromium 134 for browser checks.
The one-time scroll enhancement uses JavaScript and module-owned IDs; playback
and numerical results remain independent of it. A 60-second display clock may
have minor rendering/scheduling overhead and does not eliminate the relative
compression caused by rare late arrivals. The model is advection-only, with
idealized tubes and zero-volume mixed nodes; no diffusion, immobile exchange or
trapping mechanism is present. Internal numerical verification is not validation
against measured soil behavior. EN/DE scientific text is supplied; the other
seven languages retain the established explicit English fallback.
