The prescribed heads apply at the **centres** of the outer cells. The middle cell receives water from the left and releases water to the right. Darcy’s law gives

$$
Q_{\rm in}=C_L(h_L-h),\qquad Q_{\rm out}=C_R(h-h_R).
$$

Each face conductance is $C=K\,W\,\bar b/\Delta x$, with width $W$, saturated face thickness $\bar b$, and centre spacing $\Delta x$. The steady balance is $R(h)=Q_{\rm in}-Q_{\rm out}=0$. A positive residual means that inflow exceeds outflow. A negative residual means that outflow exceeds inflow.

The signed percentage discrepancy is $100\,R/[(Q_{\rm in}+Q_{\rm out})/2]$. “Within tolerance” in the budget view means that its magnitude is at most 0.01%. Changing homogeneous $K$ scales both discharges equally, so the head that balances them stays the same.
