### Continue with the two experiments

**Section 2 — Three-cell model, approximately 20–25 minutes.** Trace the updates from an initial head, compare the two methods, and use both head change and flow residual to assess convergence. Begin without ET, then explore what happens when updates cross an ET threshold. Use the same starting head when you want to compare the methods under matching conditions.

**Section 3 — Eleven-cell model, approximately 20–25 minutes.** Apply the same principles to nine unknown interior heads that are solved together. Relate the selected cell's diagram to the changing head profile and to convergence checks for the whole model. The selected cell is part of a coupled calculation: its neighboring heads are also unknown, so its diagram must not be interpreted as an isolated three-cell solve.

The three-cell example uses harmonic transmissivity. The eleven-cell model uses integrated saturated thickness and harmonic hydraulic conductivity to support its analytical comparison. The physical dependence on head and the iteration principles carry over, although the connection formulas differ.
