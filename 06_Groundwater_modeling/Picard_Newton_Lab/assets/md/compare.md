The figures reproduce the **parameter choices and starting heads** of the scalar demonstrations in Mehl (2006). The conductivity contrast is essential to their shape; a homogeneous aquifer produces a different Picard curve. Picard seeks an intersection with the diagonal, while Newton seeks a zero of the same balance using a tangent.

The paper uses different starting heads to illustrate the two methods. These figures therefore demonstrate their geometry, not a controlled comparison of iteration counts. The orange paths are computed from the equations, not traced from the publication. The publication gives numerical model parameters without a complete physical unit/spacing specification for these figure pairs. We apply a common SI scale to conductance and ET discharge, yielding m³/s here. This chosen rescaling leaves the roots and numerical updates unchanged; it is not a claim that the paper stated these SI dimensions.
