Source: Mehl, S. (2006), *Use of Picard and Newton Iteration for Solving Nonlinear Ground Water Flow Equations*, Ground Water 44(4), 583–594. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-6584.2006.00207.x) · [USGS record](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70028061).

This is a steady-state, one-dimensional teaching solver, not a MODFLOW executable. The three-cell experiments retain Mehl's harmonic transmissivity averaging. The eleven-cell experiment integrates saturated thickness between adjacent heads and uses harmonic hydraulic conductivity, so the homogeneous model reproduces the analytical head profile at cell centres. Both use an exact residual Jacobian. The optional ET boundary belongs to the three-cell experiments. Storage, dry-cell reactivation and automatic line search are omitted. Numerical convergence does not establish field accuracy. English and German content is available. Missing translations use English.
